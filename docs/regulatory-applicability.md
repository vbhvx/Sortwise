# Regulatory Applicability Register & Compliance Boundaries

## 1. Applicable Regulatory Frameworks

Sortwise provides built-in workflow support for the following quality management standards:

### 1.1 FDA QMSR 21 CFR 820 (Effective February 2, 2026)
- **Scope**: Medical devices and combination products.
- **Key Clauses Implemented**:
  - `820.35`: Control of records & complaint files.
  - `820.198`: Timely handling, mandatory investigations for patient safety issues, and documentation when an investigation is deemed not necessary.
  - `820.100`: Corrective and preventive action (CAPA) analysis and verification of effectiveness.

### 1.2 US FDA 21 CFR 211.198 (Pharma Complaint Files)
- **Scope**: Finished pharmaceuticals.
- **Key Clauses Implemented**:
  - Written record of all written and oral complaints.
  - Quality control unit review of any complaint involving failure of a drug product.
  - Formal documentation when an investigation is not considered necessary.

### 1.3 ISO 13485:2016
- **Scope**: Global medical device quality management systems.
- **Key Clauses Implemented**:
  - Clause 8.2.2: Complaint handling procedures and records.
  - Clause 8.2.3: Reporting to regulatory authorities.
  - Clause 8.5.2 & 8.5.3: Corrective and preventive actions with effectiveness verification.

## 2. Unverified Compliance Gaps & Non-Claims

> **CRITICAL COMPLIANCE NOTICE**:
> 
> Sortwise provides the technical controls and auditable record structures required for quality workflows. However:
> 1. Sortwise does **NOT** claim automated compliance with FDA, GMP, or EU MDR regulations.
> 2. Software alone cannot constitute compliance; compliance requires organizational validation (IQ/OQ/PQ), formal approved Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), qualified personnel, and ongoing internal quality audits.
> 3. AI-generated risk scores, duplicate candidates, and root-cause hypotheses are advisory tools only and must **never** be used as regulatory proof of product safety or cause without qualified human judgment.
